# Submission note draft — review and personalise before sending

I built a responsive, single-page website concept for AVERIQ because the assignment asks visitors to understand the company and its financial automation work quickly. The page explains both product types: end-to-end workflows such as financial audit and budget review, and focused tools such as purchase-order extraction and reconciliation. A new workflow gallery uses diagrammatic, clearly illustrative process panels instead of inventing customer case studies. I kept these categories separate so visitors can understand the difference between a complete process and a tool for one task.

My main design decision was to make professional review the central idea. The hero uses an illustrative review workspace, and an interactive capability walkthrough shows documents being read, matched, checked, and prepared as draft work. The workflow then ends with a human sign-off. This reflects the brief’s principle that software prepares the work while the professional reviews and decides. The interface adapts the Northline Studio reference dark graphite surfaces, editorial case-study layouts, lime technical diagrams, atmospheric hero lighting, and scroll motion to AVERIQ. It keeps the supplied AVERIQ mark unchanged and is responsive on desktop and mobile.

With more time, I would connect the demo request to an approved contact endpoint, review the experience with finance professionals, and refine the product explanations based on their feedback. I used Alkazim’s public site only to understand the professional-finance context; its accounting and advisory services are not presented as AVERIQ products. The current form is only a local prototype and does not send or save information. I would also complete browser-based accessibility and device checks before deployment.
